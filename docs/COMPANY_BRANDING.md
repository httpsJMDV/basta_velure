## 2. Logo & Brand Mark

### Visual Description
The Loved-IT brand mark represents warm, reliable, and accessible commerce tailored for the Filipino consumer and independent merchant. It combines a clean, human-centered heart motif (*signifying genuine customer care and malasakit*) fused seamlessly into a modern shopping bag and parcel outline.

### Logo Symbolism & Anatomy
1. **The Heart-Bag Silhouette:** Symbolizes customer delight and merchant passion — delivering goods that customers truly "love" with the peace of mind of buyer escrow protection.
2. **Bold, Rounded Geometric Typography:** A contemporary, approachable sans-serif logotype designed for crystal-clear readability across mobile devices, app icons, parcel packaging, and desktop browsers.
3. **Dual-Tone Contrast:** A balanced balance of bold crimson red and warm gold/amber accents reflecting Philippine vibrant culture and economic vitality.

### Logo Variants & Assets
* **Full Horizontal Lockup (Primary):** Features the iconic heart mark alongside the bold "Loved-IT" wordmark and "The General Marketplace" descriptor. Ideal for website navigation bars, official letterheads, and banners.
* **Stacked / Centered Lockup:** Used on splash screens, promotional banners, mobile landing views, and social media headers.
* **Icon-Only Mark (App Icon / Favicon / Avatar):** A compact circular badge housing the emblem, optimized for mobile app icons, browser favicons, browser tabs, and merchant seller badges.
* **Dark & Light Adaptations:**
  * *On Light Backgrounds:* Deep brand crimson (#A32A2A) on warm-white canvas (#FAF9F8).
  * *On Dark Backgrounds:* High-contrast vibrant white and amber-tinted emblem on dark neutral charcoal (#121212).

## 4. Short Description of the E-Commerce Business

### Elevator Pitch (Short Description)
> **Loved-IT by BASTA Corp.** is an independent Philippine multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace built to make digital trade fair, dependable, and accessible across the archipelago. From everyday household items and pantry groceries to regional artisanal crafts and cosmetics, Loved-IT connects accredited Filipino merchants with nationwide shoppers through 7-day payment escrow protection, pre-vetted FDA regulatory compliance, and transparent merchant economics.

### Expanded Business Overview

#### The Market Challenge
Filipino e-commerce shoppers frequently face unreliable product authenticity, unverified consumable goods, opaque service markups, and impersonal, bot-driven dispute systems. Meanwhile, local Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) face excessive commission take-rates and high upfront listing fees on foreign platforms.

#### The BASTA Corp. / Loved-IT Solution
Loved-IT delivers a community-first digital shopping infrastructure designed for the Philippine operating environment:
1. **Default Buyer Escrow Protection:** Customer payments (via GCash and Cash on Delivery) are held safely in a 7-day escrow account and are released to merchants only after the customer confirms the item arrived safely and as described.
2. **Pro-Seller Economics:** Fair, sustainable platform commission rates (8% for Food & Grocery, 10% standard general merchandise) with zero recurring storefront fees, enabling independent sellers to retain healthy business margins.
3. **Consumer Health & Regulatory Standards:** Mandated FDA CPR (Certificate of Product Registration) and LTO (License to Operate) verification for all consumable health, food, and cosmetic listings.
4. **48-Hour Human Dispute Resolution:** Dedicated local support team that mediates order claims and shipping exceptions with genuine *malasakit* instead of canned robotic replies.
5. **Archipelago-Wide Reach:** Streamlined logistics integration connecting Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao without inflated regional delivery surcharges.

## 6. Typography System

* **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, system `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, sans-serif
* **Headings:** Bold to Black weights (700 / 800 / 900), tight tracking (`tracking-tight`) for an impactful, modern tech presence.
* **Body Copy:** Regular and Medium weights (400 / 500) with relaxed line height (`leading-relaxed`) for effortless readability.
* **Badges & Mono Labels:** Monospace numerical badges and uppercase tracked micro-labels (`text-[10px] font-bold uppercase tracking-wider`).
