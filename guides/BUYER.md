# Buyer Guide

How to shop on Loved-IT, step by step. Everything here is in the storefront and in **Account** (`/settings`).

---

## 1. Create an account

1. Go to **Register** (`/register`).
2. Fill in your name, email, mobile number, birthday and password, then tick the Terms box.
   - Mobile: `09XXXXXXXXX` or `+639XXXXXXXXX`
   - Password: 8+ characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol
   - You must be 18 or older
3. Check your email for a **6-digit code** and enter it. The code works for 5 minutes. Tap **Resend code** for a new one.
4. Sign in at `/login` with your **email or username** and your password.

Signed up with Google? You'll be asked to add your ID and address before you can shop.

## 2. Set up your account

- **Profile** (`/settings`): name, photo, birthday. To change your email or mobile number, you confirm it with a code.
- **Addresses** (`/settings/addresses`): add where you want things delivered. Pick region → province → city → **barangay**. The barangay is required, or checkout won't work.
- **ID verification** (optional): add a government ID. The admin reviews it.

## 3. Find something to buy

1. Search, or open a category.
2. Open a product. Pick the variant (color, size), then the quantity.
3. Look out for:
   - **Sale price**: the old price is crossed out
   - **Flash sale**: a countdown timer. You can tap to get a reminder before it starts.
   - **Sold out** options are greyed out
   - A shop **on vacation** can't take orders
4. Tap **Add to cart**, or **Buy now** to go straight to checkout.

## 4. Get vouchers (optional)

1. Go to **My vouchers** (`/settings/vouchers`) → **Loved-IT vouchers** tab.
2. Tap **Claim** on the ones you want. They go into your wallet.
3. You can use at most **one store voucher per shop, one platform voucher and one shipping voucher** per checkout.

## 5. Check out

1. Open the **cart** (`/cart`). Tick the items you want to buy now.
2. Tap **Check out**.
3. Choose your **delivery address**.
4. Add vouchers if you have any. The page shows what each one takes off, or why it doesn't work (for example "spend ₱X more").
5. Add a note for a shop if you want (up to 200 characters).
6. Choose how to pay:

| Payment | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Cash on delivery** | Pay the rider in cash when it arrives. Have the exact amount ready. |
| **GCash** | Send the money to **each shop's GCash** (shown on the page), then type the **13-digit reference number** and upload the **receipt screenshot** for each shop. |
| **E-wallet or card** | After placing the order, you go to the payment page. **Pay within 1 hour**, or the order is cancelled. If it fails, tap **Pay again**. |

7. Tap **Place order**.

**Good to know:**
- A cart with items from 3 shops becomes **3 orders**, each with its own delivery fee.
- The delivery fee depends on the distance (your area vs the shop's area) and the weight.
- If a price changed while you were checking out, you'll be asked to confirm the new total.
- If no courier delivers to your address, you can't place the order. Choose another address.

## 6. Follow your order

Go to **Orders** (`/settings/orders`). The tabs are:

| Tab | Meaning |
|---|---|
| To pay | Waiting for your e-wallet or card payment |
| To ship | The shop is confirming or packing |
| To receive | On the way |
| Completed | Done |
| Cancelled | Cancelled orders |
| Return / refund | Orders with a return request |

- Tap **Track package** to see every step and a map. While a rider is bringing it, you can see the rider's first name and where they are.
- The rider might message you. Reply from **Messages** (`/settings/messages`). You can't start a chat with the rider.

## 7. Receive your order

1. When the rider arrives, take the parcel. The rider takes a photo as proof of delivery.
2. Pay the cash if it's COD.
3. Check the item, then tap **Order Received**.
   - If you don't tap it, the order completes by itself **7 days** after delivery.

## 8. Cancel an order

1. Open the order and tap **Cancel order**.
2. Pick a reason. If you choose "Other", write 10 to 300 characters.

- You can cancel only **before the shop marks it Ready for Pickup**.
- Your vouchers come back to your wallet (if they haven't expired). If you already paid, you're refunded the exact amount.

## 9. Return an item

1. Open a **delivered** order and tap **Return / refund**. You have **7 days** after delivery, and you must do it before tapping Order Received.
2. Pick a reason, describe the problem (10 to 500 characters), and add **1 to 5 photos**.
3. The shop has **3 days** to answer. **No answer means it's approved.**
4. If the shop **rejects** it, you can tap **Escalate** within 3 days, and Loved-IT decides. You can escalate only once.
5. If it's approved, you're refunded and the order becomes Returned.

## 10. Write a review

1. After the order is **Completed**, open it and tap **Rate**, or go to **Reviews** (`/settings/reviews`).
2. Give 1 to 5 stars, up to 500 characters and up to 5 photos.
3. You have **30 days**, and you can **edit once**.

## 11. Other things you can do

- **Messages**: chat with shops. You can attach an order or a product.
- **Wishlist & stores**: save items and follow shops.
- **Notification bell**: shows order updates live.
- **Become a seller**: apply from your account (see the Seller guide).
