# Rider Guide

How riders work on Loved-IT, step by step. The rider app is made for phones (`/rider`).

---

## 1. Get your account

**You can't sign up yourself.** Your courier company adds you.

1. The company enters your details, license, vehicle and plate, and photos of your license and OR/CR.
2. You get an **email link**. Open it and set your password.
   - The link works **once, for 24 hours**. If it expired, ask your company to send a new one.
   - Password: 8+ characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
3. Your account is now **Active**. Sign in at `/rider/login`.

## 2. Start your shift

1. **Share your location** when the app asks. While you're carrying parcels, the app stays locked until you share it. (Use the site on `https://`.)
2. On the **Dashboard**, switch to **Online**.
   - Only Online riders get job offers.
   - You can hold up to **3 jobs** at a time.
   - Going Offline never drops jobs you already accepted.

## 3. Get a pickup job

- When a shop has a parcel ready, the app may **offer it to you**. You have **60 seconds** to **Accept** or **Decline**.
- Offers go to the nearest free rider first (your areas cover the shop's barangay, then same city, then same province...).
- If you decline or don't answer, it goes to the next rider.
- The **Pickups** page also shows:
  - **Available**: pickups in your areas you can accept
  - **My pickups**: what you accepted
  - **Picked up**: what you're carrying to the hub

## 4. Pick up from the shop

1. Go to the shop. Tap **Message** if you need to talk to the seller.
2. Scan (or type) each parcel's **tracking number**.
3. Confirm the handover. The parcel is now **Picked Up**.
4. Bring it to your hub. When the hub scans it in, you earn **₱30**.

## 5. Collect parcels for delivery

1. The hub assigns parcels to you. They show in **Deliveries** → **To collect**.
2. At the hub, scan the **QR code** on the hub's screen (or type the tracking number).
3. The parcels move to **Out for delivery**.

## 6. Deliver

Each stop card shows the address, the buyer's phone, and the **COD amount** if it's cash on delivery. You can open **Maps** or **Message** the buyer.

**Delivered:**
1. Take a **proof photo** of the parcel handed over. A second photo is optional.
2. Ask the buyer for their **6-digit delivery code** and enter it.
   - **5 wrong codes lock code entry for 15 minutes.**
3. For COD, enter the cash you collected. It must be **exactly** the COD amount.
4. Submit. You earn **₱50**.

**Delivery failed:**
1. Pick a reason: recipient unavailable, wrong address, refused, couldn't contact, address not accessible, or other (other needs a note).
2. A failed attempt pays nothing.
3. The hub decides what happens next: another try (up to 3 in total), or a return to the seller. If the buyer **refused** it, or it was the **3rd try**, it goes back to the seller automatically.

## 7. Return trips

If your company gives you a parcel to bring **back to the seller**, it shows on the **Deliveries** page under returns. Hand it to the seller, then mark it as handed back.

## 8. Cash on delivery money

1. Open **Earnings**. The COD card shows how much cash you're holding.
2. When you hand cash to your company, enter the amount (and a reference if there is one).
3. It stays **Pending** until **your company confirms** it. Only then does your balance go down.
4. If you hand in less than you hold, the company sees a shortfall.

## 9. Earnings

Open **Earnings**:
- **Earnings tab**: ₱30 per pickup, ₱50 per delivery, by day, week and month
- **History tab**: your deliveries and failed attempts

**Your courier company pays you.** Set your GCash in **My account**.

## 10. Messages

- You can start a chat about a parcel:
  - with the **seller** during pickup
  - with the **buyer** during delivery
  - with your **hub** during both
- Buyers can't message you first.
- The chat closes when the parcel is done, but you can still read it.
- All your chats are in **My account** → **Messages**. A red badge shows unread ones.
- Limits: 1,000 characters per message, 20 messages a minute.

## 11. My account

- Your profile and hub
- **Vehicle**: changing it needs a **new OR/CR**, and your company checks it again
- **License**: renew it when it expires (the expiry must be today or later)
- **GCash** for your pay
- **Log out**
