# Seller Guide

How to sell on Loved-IT, step by step. Everything here is in the **Seller Center** (`/seller`).

---

## 1. Apply to sell

1. Go to `/register/seller`. A new visitor fills in the account and the shop in one form. A signed-in buyer fills in only the shop.
2. Fill in:
   - **Shop name**: 3 to 30 characters, at least 2 letters, not already taken
   - **Shop category**: pick a main category
   - **Seller type**, which decides the documents:

| Seller type | Documents |
|---|---|
| Individual | Government ID |
| Sole proprietorship | Government ID + DTI certificate + BIR Form 2303 |
| Corporation | Government ID + SEC certificate + BIR Form 2303 |

   - **TIN**: `000-000-000-000`. It can't already be used by another seller or courier.
   - **Pickup address**: region, province, city, **barangay**, street, 4-digit postal code. Riders pick up here.
   - Files: JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB each.
3. If you're new, enter the 6-digit email code.
4. Wait for the **admin** to approve. You'll get an email and a notification.

While you wait, you can already prepare **product drafts**. You can publish only after approval.

You stay a buyer too: the same account can still shop.

## 2. Set up your shop

1. **Shop Profile**: shop name, contact number, response time, **low-stock alert level** (5 unless you change it), and **vacation mode** (turns off ordering while you're away).
2. **Payments** → payment settings:
   - Your **GCash** number, name and QR code. Without these, buyers can't pay you by GCash.
   - Optional **free delivery minimum**: orders above this amount get free delivery.
3. **Account Settings**: password, email, mobile, notification choices (by bell and by email).

## 3. Add products

1. **Products** → **Add**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: 5 to 120 characters
   - **Description**: 20 to 3,000 characters
   - **Category**: pick a sub-category
   - **Photos**: 1 to 9, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each
   - **Weight** in kg. It sets the delivery fee.
   - **Variations** (optional): up to 2 types, like Color and Size, with up to 20 options each
   - Per variant: **price** (₱1 to ₱1,000,000) and **stock** (0 to 99,999), and an optional SKU
   - Optional **purchase limit** per buyer
   - Optional **store categories** (up to 3)
3. Save as a **draft**, or **publish**. Published products go live right away.
   - Food and similar categories need your **FDA License to Operate** and the product's **CPR/CPN** before publishing.
4. From the Products list you can edit price and stock, duplicate, and bulk activate, deactivate or delete. A product with unfinished orders can't be deleted.

**Store Categories**: make your own shop sections (2 levels). Deleting one moves its products to "Uncategorized".

## 4. Run promotions (optional)

In **Promotions**:
- **Sale**: a discount with a start and end date. One product can't have two sales at the same time.
- **Flash sale**: pick a 2-hour slot, a flash price and how many units.
- **Bundle**: buy enough of chosen products and get a percentage off.
- **Buy X get Y**: buy a set, get some units of another product free.

In **Vouchers**, make **store vouchers**:
- Code: 4 to 12 letters and numbers (saved in capitals)
- A fixed amount (no more than the minimum spend) or a percentage (1–99%, with an optional cap)
- Usage limits and dates
- Once a buyer claims it, you can only extend the end date or raise the total limit.

You pay for all promotions and store vouchers. Loved-IT pays for platform and shipping vouchers.

## 5. Handle orders

Go to **Orders**. The tabs are: **New, To Ship, Shipping, Completed, Cancelled, Return/Refund**.

1. **New order arrives.** You get a notification. (For e-wallet or card orders, you're only notified once the buyer pays.)
2. **Accept it:**
   - **COD**: accept it.
   - **GCash**: check the buyer's receipt and reference against your GCash. If it matches, accept. If not, **reject the payment** with a reason.
   - **E-wallet/card**: accept once it's paid.
3. Mark it **Preparing** while you pack.
4. Mark it **Ready for Pickup**. A **tracking number** and **waybill** are created.
5. **Print the waybill** and stick it on the parcel.
6. A rider comes, scans the tracking number and takes it. From here the courier handles it.

**Cancelling:** you can cancel until the rider picks it up. Choose a reason (out of stock, buyer asked, can't ship to the address, pricing error, or other). Stock goes back, and a paid order is refunded.

**Failed delivery coming back:** if a delivery fails for good, the parcel is brought back to you. Tap **confirm received** on the order. Only then does the stock go back on sale.

## 6. Answer returns

1. A return request shows in **Return/Refund**, with the buyer's reason and photos.
2. You have **3 days**:
   - **Approve**: the buyer is refunded and the order is Returned.
   - **Reject**: write a reason (10 to 300 characters). The buyer can escalate to Loved-IT within 3 days.
3. **No answer within 3 days means it's approved automatically.**

## 7. Reviews

In **Reviews & Ratings**, see every review of your products. You can post **one reply** per review and edit it. You can't hide or delete reviews.

## 8. Your money

### How you get paid

| Payment | Where the money goes |
|---|---|
| **GCash** | Straight to your GCash when the buyer pays |
| **COD** | The rider collects it. The courier pays you weekly (see below). |
| **E-wallet / card** | Loved-IT holds it. It becomes **Available when the order is Completed**, and then you request a payout. |

### Commission

Loved-IT takes **5%** of each item's price after your own discounts (some categories may have a different rate).

### Earnings & Payouts

1. See **Pending** (order not completed yet) and **Available** money.
2. Tap **Request payout**: at least **₱100**, no more than what's Available, to your GCash (`09XXXXXXXXX`).
3. Only **one request at a time**. The admin sends it and marks it done.

### Payments page (couriers and commission)

- **Weekly courier statement**: the cash the courier collected for you, minus the delivery fees it earned.
  - If the courier owes you, they send it and you **confirm** it arrived, or **report "not received"**.
  - If you owe the courier (for example, on GCash orders the delivery fee stays with you), you send it by GCash and record it, with a receipt.
- **Monthly commission**: pay the commission on your COD and GCash orders to Loved-IT's GCash. The admin confirms it.

## 9. Reports and messages

- **Sales Reports**: today, or any date range, with a chart.
- **Messages**: chat with buyers. Riders may message you about a pickup.
- **Dashboard**: orders, earnings and low-stock items at a glance.
