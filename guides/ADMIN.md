# Admin Guide

How to run Loved-IT as an admin, step by step. Everything here is in the **Admin panel** (`/admin`). Admin accounts are made by the team; there is no admin sign-up.

---

## 1. Daily check

1. Open the **Dashboard**. It shows the last 30 days: sales, commission, orders, active buyers and sellers, and the order pipeline.
2. Check the **notification bell** for new applications and escalated returns.
3. Work through the lists below that have items waiting.

## 2. Approve applications

| Page | What to check | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Seller Applications** | Shop name, category, TIN, address, and the documents for their seller type (ID; plus DTI or SEC, and BIR 2303 for businesses) | Approve or reject. Approved buyers become sellers. |
| **Buyer Applications** | The buyer's government ID (front and back), birthday and address | Approve or reject |
| **Courier Applications** | Company name, SEC number, TIN, contact person, regions served, business permit, SEC certificate, BIR 2303, representative's ID | Approve or reject. You can also **suspend** or **reactivate** a courier later. |
| **Hub Applications** | Hub details, DTI permit, owner's ID | Usually the **courier company** decides. You can step in as a backup. |

Applications reach you only after the person has verified their email.

## 3. Manage people

- **Sellers**, **Buyers**, **Riders**: search and open accounts. You can **suspend** or **reactivate** them. A suspended account can't sign in.
- Riders are added and managed by their courier company. You only oversee them.

## 4. Products and categories

- **Products**: see every listing. Sellers' products go live when they publish. You can:
  - **archive** a product (take it down) or **reactivate** it
  - **approve** or **reject** listings waiting for review (a rejection needs a reason)
  - open a food product's FDA papers
- **Categories**:
  - create, edit, turn on or off, move, and delete categories
  - set a **commission rate** for a category (at most 2 decimals). A sub-category without one uses its parent's rate, then the platform rate.
  - a category with products (archived ones included) can't be deleted

## 5. Orders

In **Orders**:
- See every order, with figures and status chips. Search by order number, tracking number, buyer or shop.
- Open an order to see its full history and who did each step.

## 6. Payments & Payouts

| Tab | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Payouts** | Sellers request payouts (for e-wallet and card orders). Send the money to their GCash, then tap **mark sent** → **complete**, or **reject**. |
| **Commission** | Each month, shops pay the commission on their COD and GCash orders to Loved-IT's GCash. Check that it arrived, then **confirm** it. Set Loved-IT's GCash here. |
| **Settlements** | Weekly payments between couriers and shops that went wrong: reported "not received", not confirmed within 3 days, or voided. Check both GCash accounts, the reference and the receipt, then mark it **received** or ask them to **pay again**. You can send a reminder once a day. |

## 7. Disputes / Returns

Only **escalated** returns come to you (the shop rejected the return and the buyer escalated).
1. Open the case. See the buyer's reason and photos, and the shop's reason, side by side.
2. Decide:
   - **Refund**: the buyer gets their money back, and the order is Returned.
   - **Reject**: the order completes.
3. Write a note of at least 10 characters.

## 8. Vouchers

In **Vouchers**, make **platform vouchers** (money off the order) and **shipping vouchers** (money off delivery). Loved-IT pays for these.
- Code: 4 to 12 letters and numbers, unique
- A fixed amount (no more than the minimum spend) or a percentage (1–99%, with an optional cap)
- Usage limits (the per-buyer limit can't be more than the total), and start and end dates
- Once a buyer claims one, you can only extend the end date or raise the total limit.

## 9. Reviews & Feedback

- **Reviews**: read reviews by shop. This is **read-only**: reviews can't be hidden or deleted.
- **Feedback**: messages people sent about the site.

## 10. Reports

**Analytics & Reports**: pick any date range to see sales, commission, orders, payments and more. Cancelled and returned orders aren't counted in sales.

## 11. Platform Settings

- **Base commission** (5% by default)
- **Commission per category**
- **Return rules**: the return window (7 days), the shop's days to answer (3), the buyer's days to escalate (3), and the return policy text
- **Payment methods**: COD, GCash to the shop, and e-wallet and card

Changes are saved to the Activity Log.

## 12. Messages and Activity Log

- **Messages**: chat with buyers and sellers. (Rider chats are private, so admins can't see them.)
- **Activity Log**: every admin action, grouped by day. Search and filter by area, admin and date.
